Artist Bio

P1P3 is a DJ focused on creating immersive sonic experiences within Melodic Techno and Peak Time Underground. His style is built on layering — maintaining continuous flow with clean transitions and a narrative that evolves constantly throughout each set.

His approach blends progressive energy construction, depth through layering, precise transitions, and a musical selection oriented towards atmosphere and emotional impact.

"Dive into the sound" — an invitation to enter a fully immersive sonic experience.
