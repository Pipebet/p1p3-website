ABOUT

PEAK TIME 137–140 BPM MELODIC PROGRESSIVE 130–132 BPM HARD DRIVE / HARDGROOVE 140–142 BPM

P1P3 is a DJ specialised in high-energy experiences within underground electronic music. His sound moves between Peak Time Techno (137–140 BPM), powerful Melodic Progressive (130–132 BPM) and Hard Drive / Hardgroove inspired by the energy of the contemporary European scene (140–142 BPM).

His sets are defined by dynamic mixing, precise transitions and a progressive tension build that keeps the dancefloor in constant motion. Influenced by both modern techno and the rhythmic force of 2000s sounds, he develops sessions where energy and groove are the protagonists.

P1P3 has featured on the international platform HÖR Berlin — one of the most recognised showcases for emerging and established artists in the global electronic scene. Through this exposure, a significant part of his digital audience comes from Germany, which concentrates a major share of his SoundCloud plays.
